# Onboarding Quickstart

> SYNTHETIC sample document created by the team for development and testing.
> Orbitly Inc. is a fictional company. Last updated: 2026-06-10.

## Create a workspace

Sign up at the Orbitly website with a work email address. The first user becomes the workspace Owner.
New workspaces start with a 14-day free trial of the Standard plan. No credit card is required for the trial.

## Invite your team

Open Admin > Users > Invite and enter email addresses, one per line. Invitations expire after 7 days.
You can resend an expired invitation from the same page.

## Seat limits

The Standard plan supports up to 50 paid seats per workspace.
The Enterprise plan has no seat limit.
To add seats, the Owner opens Billing > Seats and increases the seat count; charges are prorated.

## Install the desktop app

The desktop app is available for Windows 10 or later and macOS 13 or later.
Linux is supported through the web app only.

## Next steps

Configure single sign-on (see the SSO Administration Guide) and connect integrations such as Slack and Jira.
