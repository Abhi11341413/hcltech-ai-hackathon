SYNTHETIC sample document created by the team for development and testing.

Signing in

Sign in at the login page with your work email. If your organisation uses single sign-on (SSO), choose Continue with SSO and you will be redirected to your identity provider.

Password reset

To reset your password, open Settings > Security and choose Reset password. A link valid for 30 minutes is emailed to you.

Multi-factor authentication

Administrators can require multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all users from Admin > Security policies. Supported methods are authenticator apps and hardware keys.
