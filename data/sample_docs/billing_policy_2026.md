# Billing Policy (2026)

> SYNTHETIC sample document created by the team for development and testing.
> Effective from 1 January 2026.

## Invoices

Invoices are issued on the first business day of each month and are available under Billing > Invoices.

## Refunds

Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refundable.

## Payment methods

We accept credit cards and bank transfer. Bank transfer is available for Enterprise plans only.
