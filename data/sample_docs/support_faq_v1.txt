Last updated: 2025-03-15

LIMITS
Q: What is the API rate limit?
A: Each account is limited to 100 requests per minute.

Q: What happens if I exceed the limit?
A: Further requests are rejected with HTTP 429 until the next minute.

BILLING
Q: When are invoices issued?
A: Invoices are issued on the first business day of each month.

Q: Can I get a refund?
A: Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase for annual plans.
